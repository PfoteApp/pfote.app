Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Pfote doesn’t collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer never sees your information.

Where Your Data Is

Pets, medications, dose history, weights and photos are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they’re also kept in your private iCloud account and synced between your own devices.

Apple protects this data, and nobody else can read it.

Medication Descriptions

To show a short description of a medication, its name (or the search name you enter) is sent to Wikipedia and Wikidata. Nothing else is sent.

You can hide descriptions anytime.

Backups, PDFs and Sharing

Backups, overviews and medical history PDFs are only created when you ask for them, and go only where you send them.

The name you enter under “Your Name” is saved with the doses you log, so everyone caring for your pets can see who gave a dose. It stays in your own data.

Permissions

Notifications and AlarmsFor dose and refill reminders.
Camera and PhotosOnly for pictures you choose.
Face IDTo lock the app, if you turn that on.

All of this stays on your device.

Children

Pfote isn’t directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
